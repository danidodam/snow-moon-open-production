# Scene breakdown

## SC01

- **Story beat:** **TITLE: A WALK THROUGH KALIMAR**

**SETTING:** Kalimar District, Meldan, the capital city of Veridia.

**SCENE ATMOSPHERE:** A peaceful, tree-lined footpath in a modern city. Tall trees stand on both sides of the path, partially concealing medieval-style stone houses. The surroundings are clean, natural, and quiet. Scattered leaves, plants, and trees blend seamlessly with the architecture. Small commercial signs hang on some houses.

**CHARACTERS:**
- **GLADIAS** — A man walking through Kalimar.
- **BOY** — A young child playing with a flying toy.
- **GRANDMOTHER** — An elderly woman accompanied by a small child.
- **COUPLE** — Two people seated on a bench.
- **EMERALD** — The local AI assistant, heard through Gladias's hand device.
- **DRONE** — A small spherical toy with feather-like rotors on three sides.
- **FAMILY** — A family with four children, including the boy and two younger children riding tricycles.

---

### SCENE ONE: THE FOOTPATH OF KALIMAR

**[LIGHTS UP.]**

**[STAGE DIRECTIONS:]**

A footpath stretches across the stage.

Tall trees tower on both sides of the path, their branches and foliage creating a dense, natural canopy.

Behind the trees, on either side, stand a series of large stone-brick houses. Their architecture is medieval in style, yet their appearance is immaculate. There is no visible junk, no dust on the ground, and none of the imperfections one would expect from medieval structures a thousand years old.

The houses appear to meld seamlessly into the surrounding vegetation. Scattered leaves lie among plants and trees, blending the natural environment with the stone architecture.

A few houses feature small signs advertising clothing, flowers, and food for sale.

The footpath is quiet.

**[GLADIAS ENTERS, WALKING ALONG THE FOOTPATH.]**

He walks at a relaxed pace, looking around at the scenery.

He passes beneath the tall trees, with the stone houses partially visible behind the foliage on either side.

He slows.

He looks around.

He takes a deep breath, pausing briefly to appreciate the natural surroundings.

**[GLADIAS STOPS WALKING.]**

For a moment, he stands quietly, taking in the peaceful environment.

The trees, plants, scattered leaves, and stone houses surround him. The atmosphere is dense with greenery and quiet enough that he almost forgets where he is.

**[A BRIEF PAUSE.]**

**[STAGE DIRECTIONS:]**

Gladias is standing in the middle of the Kalimar district of Meldan, the capital city of Veridia, although the peaceful surroundings almost make him forget it.

**[SUDDENLY, THE SOUND OF A SMALL DRONE APPROACHING IS HEARD.]**

A small drone flies over Gladias's head.

**[GLADIAS INSTINCTIVELY LOOKS AT HIS WATCH.]**

He checks it.

Almost immediately, a green checkmark lights up on the watch's display.

**[STAGE DIRECTIONS:]**

The green checkmark appears as the watch completes a zero-knowledge authentication protocol. The watch receives a cryptographic attestation verifying two things: the drone is a toy, and its navigation algorithm contains a safety override that prevents it from coming within five decimeters of a human without permission.

The watch learns that the drone is not a threat—and nothing else.

**[GLADIAS LOOKS AT THE GREEN CHECKMARK.]**

The drone continues flying overhead.

Gladias's watch has verified the drone's safety without learning anything beyond what is necessary to establish that it is not a threat.

**[THE DRONE FLIES OFF.]**

It continues above a sidewalk branching off to the left.

**[LIGHTING AND STAGE FOCUS SHIFT SLIGHTLY TOWARD THE LEFT-BRANCHING SIDEWALK.]**

For a brief moment, Gladias can see the drone's shape clearly.

It is a small ball, with feather-like rotors on three sides.

**[A YOUNG BOY SUDDENLY JUMPS UP.]**

The boy reaches toward the drone and grabs it with his hand while it is still in the sky.

**BOY:**  
"Five points for me!"

**[THE BOY REACTS WITH EXCITEMENT.]**

**[STAGE DIRECTIONS:]**

The boy is part of a family with four children. The two younger children are each riding a tricycle.

The family remains in the surrounding area as the scene continues.

**[GLADIAS TURNS HIS ATTENTION BACK TO HIS SURROUNDINGS.]**

He resumes peacefully observing the environment around him.

---

### SCENE TWO: THE BENCH

**[STAGE FOCUS SHIFTS TOWARD THE RIGHT-BRANCHING SIDEWALK.]**

On another sidewalk, branching off to the right, a grandmother walks with a small child.

**[THE GRANDMOTHER AND CHILD APPROACH A BENCH.]**

A couple is already sitting on the bench.

As the grandmother and child approach and appear to stop, the couple anticipates their need for a seat.

**[THE COUPLE PRE-EMPTIVELY STANDS UP.]**

They rise from the bench to offer their seats to the grandmother and the small child.

**[THE GRANDMOTHER ACKNOWLEDGES THEIR GESTURE.]**

**GRANDMOTHER:**  
"Thank you."

**[SHE SITS DOWN WITH THE CHILD.]**

The couple has offered its seats before being asked.

The grandmother settles onto the bench with the small child.

**[THE SCENE REMAINS CALM AND PEACEFUL.]**

### SCENE THREE: EMERALD'S EXPLANATION

**[THROUGHOUT THE ENTIRE WALK, AN AUDIO PROGRAM HAS BEEN PLAYING INTO GLADIAS'S EAR.]**

**[STAGE DIRECTIONS:]**

The audio comes from Emerald, the local AI running on Gladias's hand device.

As Gladias walks through Kalimar, stops to appreciate the scenery, checks his watch, observes the drone, and looks at the people around him, Emerald continues explaining the subject through his ear.

**EMERALD (AUDIO):**  
An explanation of the nuances of airborne virus transmission and how to calculate the effect of changes to a building's ventilation or density on total city-wide cases during an epidemic.

**[STAGE DIRECTIONS:]**

Emerald's explanation continues as part of the scene, accompanying Gladias's walk through the district.

The audio program addresses airborne virus transmission and the calculations used to determine how changes in ventilation or building density might affect the total number of epidemic cases across the city.

Gladias continues to experience the peaceful surroundings of Kalimar while the AI's explanation plays into his ear.

**[LIGHTS SLOWLY FADE.]**

**END OF SCENE.**


# SCENE: THE SKY BRIDGE AND THE AESTHETICS VOTE

**SETTING:** Veridia. A pedestrian route leading from residential houses through thick green shrubbery, up a gently rising footpath, and onto a sky bridge above a busy road. Beyond the bridge, the road leads into a more densely built-up district. The scene ends at the entrance to a concert venue.

**CHARACTERS:**
- **GLADIAS** — A citizen of Veridia and an Acolyte in the Order of Steering.
- **EMERALD** — Gladias's AI assistant, communicating through the silken cloth band around his neck and his hand device.

**ATMOSPHERE:** A beautiful natural landscape gradually gives way to a carefully designed urban environment. The atmosphere is cool and breezy on the sky bridge, with the sounds and movements of traffic below. The scene then becomes quieter along a tree-lined pedestrian route protected by a soundproof barrier.

---

## SHOT 1: THE GREEN PATH

**CAMERA:** A medium-wide tracking shot follows Gladias as he walks forward along a footpath.

The surrounding houses gradually disappear from view, replaced by thick green shrubbery on both sides of the path.

The camera follows him through the greenery.

As the shrubbery clears, the footpath begins to curve gently upward.

The camera moves slightly ahead of Gladias, revealing the path as it rises.

Without any abrupt physical break, the footpath transitions smoothly into a sky bridge suspended above a busy road.

**SOUND:** The ambient sounds of the surrounding environment gradually give way to the sounds of traffic below.

Cars and autobuses move along the road. A few autonomous delivery vehicles pass among them.

A chilly breeze blows directly into Gladias's face.

His clothing and hair respond subtly to the wind.

He slows his pace.

## SHOT 2: THE SKY BRIDGE

**CAMERA:** A medium shot captures Gladias standing on the sky bridge.

He turns his attention toward the view.

**GLADIAS** *(out of habit, whispering so softly that only his throat muscles move; no sound leaves his lips)*:
"Pause, I want to enjoy the view."

**STAGE DIRECTION:**

Gladias's lips remain still. His throat muscles move subtly as he forms the sentence.

The silken cloth band tightly covering his neck picks up the whispered communication perfectly.

He stops in the middle of the sky bridge.

He turns to his left and looks forward.

**CAMERA:** Over-the-shoulder shot from behind Gladias, revealing the view ahead.

The busy road stretches into the distance for approximately two kilometres before turning rightward.

Beyond that point stands a mountain covered with trees.

A few apartment buildings, each approximately fifteen storeys tall, rise above the trees along the lower half of the mountain slope.

Their designs resemble oversized medieval castle towers, though their sides contain far more windows than traditional castle towers.

The buildings are visible in several places along the slope.

**CAMERA:** Hold on the landscape long enough for the audience to register the road, mountain, trees, and distinctive buildings.

The chilly breeze continues to blow.

Gladias remains still, taking in the view.

He turns his face to the left once again and takes one last look at the natural world.

**GLADIAS — INNER MONOLOGUE:**
*The nature in Veridia is beautiful.*

He then turns his face to the right.

**CAMERA:** Follow the direction of his gaze.

On this side, the road continues beyond the sky bridge into an area with a much higher density of buildings.

The natural landscape gradually gives way to the built-up urban environment.

**GLADIAS — INNER MONOLOGUE:**
*Today, his job was to offer opinions on the human world.*

## SHOT 3: THE TREE-LINED STREET

Gladias begins walking forward once more.

**CAMERA:** A tracking shot follows him as he steps off the sky bridge.

The environment on either side of the road has changed.

On his left is a row of five-storey apartment buildings, interspersed with walking paths and small shops selling various kinds of goods.

**CAMERA:** A wide shot establishes the contrast between the buildings and shops on the left and the heavily tree-screened soundproof barrier on the right.

Gladias continues walking beside the barrier.

The trees form a thick green corridor along the street.

The atmosphere is calmer here than on the sky bridge.

**SOUND:** A brief electronic buzz from Gladias's hand device.

**GLADIAS — INNER MONOLOGUE:**
*This was quadratic voting, he remembered.*

*All his votes would be automatically shifted, stretched or squeezed so that the total average of the votes becomes zero, and the average of the squares of the votes becomes one.*

*So there was no point in voting up on everything, down on everything, or overly extreme on everything.*

**GLADIAS — INNER MONOLOGUE:**

**GLADIAS — INNER MONOLOGUE:**
*Maybe it's okay to have in what was already a corporate district.*

He stops himself.

**GLADIAS — INNER MONOLOGUE:**
*Then he reminded himself that he was not voting on the aeroplane building, he was voting on Badra St #1103.*

Gladias looks at the blue-grey apartment building again.

**GLADIAS — INNER MONOLOGUE:**
*Right. What decision to make? Support or oppose?*

He studies the building for another moment.

Finally, he decides by raw instinct.

**SOUND:** A subtle confirmation sound from the hand device.

Gladias lowers the device.

**GLADIAS — INNER MONOLOGUE:**

**GLADIAS — INNER MONOLOGUE:**
*That vote had been hard.*

**GLADIAS — INNER MONOLOGUE:**
*The vote that he was about to do would be far more challenging — and there, the system would be keeping score.*

# DRAMA SCENE 2: THE AUDIT AT DREADKNOT'S CONCERT

Genre: Science fiction · Civic drama · Psychological tension

Setting: The entrance gate and main concert area in Veridia. The concert takes place in a large open field enclosed by a high soundproof barrier. Dreadknot is performing on stage.

Characters: Gladias, Emerald AI, the gate's verification machine, Dreadknot's lead singer, concert attendees, and the Acolytes participating in the audit through their devices.

## SCENE 1 — THE GATE

SHOT 1 — CLOSE-UP: THE ENTRANCE GATE

A circle with a glowing green outline is embedded in the entrance gate.

Gladias approaches, wearing his loose-fitting, dark purple Veridian Privacy Robe. The hood covers his head, and the robe extends down to his ankles. His shoes are also dark purple.

He raises his hand device and holds it near the glowing circle.

SOUND: A faint electronic hum from the gate.

Gladias waits briefly.

His hand device produces a cryptographic proof. The machine embedded in the entrance gate verifies it.

A short pause.

GATE MACHINE: "Verified, welcome."

The gate processes the credential.

It learns that someone holding a valid and unused credential has arrived—and nothing else.

Gladias passes through the gate.

CAMERA: Follow Gladias from behind as he enters the concert grounds and heads toward the main concert area.

## SCENE 2 — INSIDE THE CONCERT GROUNDS

SHOT 2 — WIDE ESTABLISHING SHOT

Gladias emerges into a large open field surrounded by a high soundproof barrier.

It is the same barrier he saw from the outside while walking along the street, now viewed from the inside.

The field is occupied by concert attendees.

A few wear the same signature Veridian Privacy Robe as Gladias: loose-fitting, dark purple garments covering the entire body from the hood to the ankles, paired with dark purple shoes.

Most of the other attendees wear simple shirts. Some are plain-colored, while others feature slogans of their favorite bands.

The crowd moves in different ways.

Some attendees walk forward with clear intent and efficiency, just like Gladias.

Others dance.

Some chat with each other.

Still others walk forward calmly, simply trying to passively enjoy the show.

Gladias moves through the crowd.

SOUND: The concert grows progressively louder as he approaches the main performance area.

## SCENE 3 — THE PRIVACY ROBE

SHOT 3 — MEDIUM TRACKING SHOT

Gladias pushes through the crowd.

The music grows louder around him.

He reaches for a strap on the front face cover of his privacy robe.

CAMERA: CLOSE-UP of his hand pulling the strap.

He tightens the belt separating the area below his nose from the area above his nose.

The movement is deliberate.

Only minutes earlier, he had been listening to a program on airborne virus transmission. The program has primed him to take care not to get sick.

He finishes adjusting the face cover and continues toward the stage.

## SCENE 4 — DREADKNOT ON STAGE

SHOT 4 — STAGE REVEAL

## SCENE 5 — WHY GLADIAS IS HERE

SHOT 5 — MEDIUM CLOSE-UP

Gladias holds his hand device and looks at its screen.

Around him, the audience continues to enjoy the music and spend time with friends.

For Gladias, this is certainly not his ideal entertainment.

His preferred music is much softer and gentler.

But he does not dislike the concert either.

More than anything else, he finds it interesting in an anthropological way: an opportunity to explore the interests and emotions of people with whom he would otherwise have little interaction.

His attention returns to his device.

GLADIAS — INNER MONOLOGUE:

But ultimately, Gladias was at the concert for quite a different purpose.

He opens the rubric on his hand device.

## SCENE 10 — THE OTHER ACOLYTES

SHOT 10 — DEVICE SCREEN

Nine Acolytes have been assigned to the audit, divided into three groups of three.

Gladias reads their messages.

He sighs.

GLADIAS — INNER MONOLOGUE:

The other two groups were probably thinking the same way.

He continues thinking through the possibilities.

If we simplify and say every other Acolyte was a coin-flip between Tier 3 and Tier 4, then by the binomial formula, the probability the other six would also be evenly split was six choose three — 20 — divided by 64, or about thirty-one percent.

He pauses to consider the calculation.

Now if the other Acolytes' votes were not coin-flips ... eh, calculating that exactly is too hard, but it's definitely lower, let's just say twenty percent.

He looks at the device.

\* A twenty or thirty percent chance his vote would significantly affect Dreadknot's tax rate for the next year.\*

A short pause.

Oh great. Actual responsibility.

## SCENE 11 — THE DECISION

SHOT 11 — WIDE SHOT OF THE CROWD

Gladias looks up from his hand device and surveys the crowd once more.

Dreadknot finishes the first song.

SOUND: The audience erupts into cheers.

Gladias stands among them, watching.

For a brief moment, the atmosphere of the concert gives him courage.

CAMERA: Return to a close-up of Gladias's hand device.

His available choices are the five tiers of the social impact rubric.

He considers the possible consequences.

His prediction score is ninety two.

A decision that differs from the Sentinels' eventual judgement could put his career on hold.

Nevertheless, he has made up his mind.

GLADIAS — INNER MONOLOGUE:

He would take the ten percent chance of a hit to his prediction score.

Without thinking further, Gladias presses Tier 3.

CAMERA: Extreme close-up of his finger selecting Tier 3 on the device.

He confirms the selection.

For a brief moment, he feels proud of his decision.

The crowd continues cheering after the first song.

Gladias remains among them, his hand device still in his possession.

Then—

VOICE FROM BEHIND GLADIAS:

"HEY EVERYONE, IT'S AN ORDER MEMBER!"

CAMERA: Gladias reacts to the shout.

CUT TO BLACK.

END OF SCENE.



# DRAMA SCENE 3: THE ESCAPE

Setting: Inside the concert venue, immediately after someone shouts, “HEY EVERYONE, IT’S AN ORDER MEMBER!”

## SCENE 1 — THE MOMENT OF SHOCK

INT. CONCERT VENUE — CONTINUOUS

The concert crowd surrounds Gladias. The sound of the venue continues around him.

Gladias’s body freezes in shock for a moment.

Before his mind can catch up, his eyes have already scanned the venue. They find the nearest exit.

He immediately understands what has happened.

The security of the Order, particularly the prevention of influence and bribery, rests on the privacy of its members. Order members are strictly forbidden from revealing their assigned task.

To ensure that Order members take their privacy seriously, the Order keeps them on their toes through a deliberately engineered game.

Anyone can connect to the decentralized cryptographic network that manages the Order’s operations and submit a guess about a particular Order member’s assigned task, along with a small deposit. If the guess is correct, the Order member’s salary is docked, and the person who made the discovery receives half as a reward.

Gladias has barely processed the situation when another man nearby, also wearing a Veridian Privacy Robe, suddenly breaks into a run.

## SCENE 2 — THE DECOY

INT. CONCERT VENUE — CONTINUOUS

The other robed man runs toward the fire exit on the right.

Immediately, everyone within ten meters takes out their hand devices and points them at him, recording video.

Gladias understands what has happened here, too.

A decoy.

Someone from the public, acting altruistically, has deliberately diverted attention toward himself to give Gladias a chance to escape.

Gladias turns around, intending to run.

But before he can take a step, he sees a large, burly man standing directly in front of him.

The man points a hand device straight at Gladias’s face.

Gladias freezes.

## SCENE 3 — A DIFFERENT GLADIAS

INT. CONCERT VENUE — CONTINUOUS

For a moment, Gladias thinks he is done for.

His mind races through the possibilities, searching for any possible way out.

Then something changes.

Gladias the thoughtful philosopher gives way to a different Gladias: more imaginative, more mischievous.

A Gladias who has not been awake since recess in middle school.

He looks toward the right.

GLADIAS “Wait, is that a new screed from the Arctic Emperor?”

The burly man looks confused.

He turns his attention toward whatever Gladias appears to be looking at.

In that instant, Gladias snatches the man’s hand device from his arm and suddenly turns around.

## SCENE 4 — DELETE AND REBOOT

INT. CONCERT VENUE — CONTINUOUS

With the device in his possession, Gladias gives a command.

GLADIAS “Delete all audio, images and video from the last longhour. Reboot.”

He begins to run.

A few ticks later, the device responds.

HAND DEVICE “4 files deleted.”

The screen briefly displays screenshots of some photos and videos.

In the bottom-right corner is a photograph of Gladias’s face.

Then the screen turns black.

## SCENE 5 — THE THROW

INT. CONCERT VENUE — CONTINUOUS

Gladias throws the hand device up into the air.

He hopes that his pursuer will choose not to lose his device rather than continue chasing him.

Gladias keeps running.

His shoes slow him down. They have high heels designed to raise his height to the same level as other privacy-robe wearers, and deliberately uneven soles designed to randomize his gait.

Despite this, he manages to reach the exit quickly.

The crowd remains distracted by the commotion. It seems not to notice him.

## SCENE 6 — THE EXIT

EXT. CONCERT VENUE — CONTINUOUS

Gladias exits the concert venue.

He hops onto a local autobus.

CUT.



# DRAMA SCENE 4: THE RIDE HOME

Setting: Inside a local Veridian autobus, immediately after Gladias escapes the concert venue and boards the vehicle.

## SCENE 1 — THE NEARLY EMPTY AUTOBUS

INT. LOCAL AUTOBUS — CONTINUOUS

The autobus is nearly empty.

Gladias sits among the few passengers.

To his left are two other people, both wearing Veridian Privacy Robes. Their loose-fitting, dark purple robes cover their bodies from their hoods down to their ankles, with matching dark purple shoes below.

Gladias cannot tell whether they are male or female, young or old.

To his right sits a teenager wearing ordinary clothes and carrying a backpack. The teenager appears to be coming home from school.

Gladias’s eyes wander to the advertisements on the inside walls of the autobus.

## SCENE 2 — THE LIFELONG LEARNING ADVERTISEMENT

INT. LOCAL AUTOBUS — CONTINUOUS

Gladias looks at the first advertisement.

It is a poster reminding Veridians of the importance of lifelong learning.

On the left side of the poster is a picture of a classroom. A child, a middle-aged woman, and a much older man sit together, all reading textbooks.

On the right side of the poster is the text:

School lasts until age 20. Education is every Veridian's responsibility and duty for a lifetime.  Learn about Veridian Graph Funding-subsidized education programs for all ages today.

Gladias looks at the poster.

## SCENE 3 — THE HYDRAFILL ADVERTISEMENT

INT. LOCAL AUTOBUS — CONTINUOUS

Gladias’s attention moves to the second advertisement.

It is for Hydrafill, a brand that sells water and various kinds of drinks. Over the last twenty years, gently nudged by Veridian Steering taxes, the company’s drinks have become almost completely sugar-free.

On the left side of the advertisement is the classic yellow-and-green Hydrafill bottle.

On the right side is the text:

## Hydrafill

Our drinks are proudly free of over 2,000 known poisons and anti-recommended substances.

Gladias smiles.

## SCENE 4 — THE VOTE HE WISHES HE HAD RECEIVED

INT. LOCAL AUTOBUS — CONTINUOUS

Gladias continues looking at the Hydrafill advertisement.

He wishes he had been selected to vote on the Hydrafill advertisement instead of the blue-and-grey building.

He knows what he would have done.

He would have slid the slider almost all the way to the right.

CUT.
