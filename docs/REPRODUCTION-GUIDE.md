# Reproduction guide

This guide is intended to help another creator understand and reproduce parts of the *Snow Moon* workflow.

## Before you begin

- Read the license and attribution notes.
- Confirm you have access to the listed tools and models.
- Check whether referenced assets are included or externally hosted.
- Review each asset's reuse terms.

## Reproduce a scene

1. Open `02-screenplay/` and identify the scene ID.
2. Find the matching storyboard in `04-storyboards/`.
3. Find the prompt entry in `05-prompts/` using the shot's prompt ID.
4. Review the tool/model version and settings.
5. Generate or source the permitted inputs.
6. Compare the result against the documented continuity and quality-control checks.
7. Find the clip manifest entry in `07-generated-media/`.
8. Follow `09-post-production-capcut/EDITING-WORKFLOW-TEMPLATE.md` for assembly.

## What may not reproduce exactly?

Generative models can produce different outputs even with similar prompts. Hosted models may change, seeds may be unavailable, and commercial tools may require paid access. Document these limitations honestly.
