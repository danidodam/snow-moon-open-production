## Quality-control checks

- Character appearance and clothing continuity
- Scene geography and screen direction
- Correct number of people/characters in frame
- Lip-sync and dialogue continuity, where relevant
- Audio levels and clipping
- Flicker, duplicate people, malformed hands/faces, and visual artifacts
- Export resolution, frame rate, duration, and playback
